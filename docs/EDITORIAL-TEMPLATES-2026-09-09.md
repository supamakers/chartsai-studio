# Editorial templates: evidence, scope and measurement

Research date: September 9, 2026. User approved 8–12 excellent examples, three collections and the exact-design replacement journey. This release supplies 12 templates on three collection URLs; there are no individual template SEO pages or indexed parameter permutations.

## Evidence and decision

Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, Google US, authenticated devgarage profile: `dumbbell chart examples` has no stored keyword/SERP data, 0–10 US/global volume and unknown KD; `small multiples examples` shows 0–10 US/global and unavailable KD; `slopegraph examples` is not indexed in the database. Ahrefs still displays its partial ranking-data repair notice. These exact phrases are not validated high-volume acquisition opportunities. Broader chart-topic evidence is in EDITORIAL-RESEARCH-2026-09-09.md; do not sum overlapping terms or forecast visits.

Actual result formats were checked: [UC Davis](https://datavizidealab.ucdavis.edu/sm-multiples) offers a small-multiples explanation and downloadable template; [Highcharts](https://www.highcharts.com/docs/chart-and-series-types/dumbbell-series) demonstrates time/group dumbbell comparisons; [Vizzlo](https://help.vizzlo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013643820-How-to-create-a-Slopegraph-with-Vizzlo) teaches slopegraphs through values and worked comparisons. Their existence supports the explanatory/template format, not an assertion of attainable rankings. No code, datasets or visual assets were copied from them.

Decision: publish three substantive browse/learn collections supporting the existing makers and broader topics. Collections explain how examples differ, expose exact values, include interpretation/caveats, and ship reusable files. Their distinct job is choosing and understanding a worked comparison; maker URLs own creation intent. Broader rankings and exact-query demand remain hypotheses. Review Search Console evidence for overlap before adding more pages.

## Content and handoff

Four templates each for dumbbell, slopegraph and small multiples. Three use the existing pinned World Bank selection, explicitly linked to its full provenance page. Nine use original fictional teaching values, labelled in both pages and exports; those fictional data are dedicated to CC0. Charts and code retain the repository's applicable license, and World Bank data retain CC BY 4.0. Files: editable SupaMakers project JSON, source CSV, SVG, PNG and raster PDF.

Fixed `?template=` IDs open exact projects in the matching maker; unknown or mismatched IDs do not load another chart. The query is canonicalized to the maker and removed from page analytics. Importing replacement data keeps palette, canvas, axis/decimal/label preferences and removes unchanged sample text/units plus old annotations. Explicit user text survives. Reopened arbitrary projects retain their own metadata and reset session template attribution. No template name or private data is inferred from filenames.

## Plausible

Template Selected is a new fixed event with tool and allowlisted template. Gallery “Use this chart” links and editor dropdown choices trigger selection; loading a query URL alone does not double-count a click. Chart Download includes the same fixed template for direct asset clicks and subsequent exports during that maker session, including after data replacement. Choosing another example changes/resets attribution. Restoring a saved project resets it; this is session lineage, not persistent person tracking or a cross-session attribution model.

Only IDs in `src/lib/editorial-template-ids.ts` are accepted, and the template must match its chart kind. Page URLs omit query/hash. Tracking is not proof of saving, publishing or downstream reuse. Test intercepted production events without creating fake usage in Plausible. The Template Selected custom-event goal and template custom property were configured and verified in the chartsai.com Plausible settings on September 9, 2026. This verifies reporting configuration, not organic usage.
