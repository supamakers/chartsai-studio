# Free tools and SupaMakers authority

Updated September 8, 2026. The user's aim is a growing collection of genuinely useful free tools that earn search discovery, AI citations, reuse, and recognition for SupaMakers. Quantity follows usefulness and evidence.

## Current expansion

The line graph maker launched with ChartsAI v0.3 on September 8, 2026. The September 7 Ahrefs research recorded Google US volume 6,000/month, global volume 20,000, and KD 13 for `line graph maker`. These are estimates, not visits. The SERP included linegraphmaker.co, Virtual Science Teachers, and linegraphmaker.me; the leading result had hundreds of referring domains. A visible Ahrefs ranking-data disruption notice limits confidence.

A September 8 review of [Line Graph Maker](https://linegraphmaker.co/) and [MakeChart](https://www.makechart.co/line-chart-generator/) confirmed the expected interactive editor and downloadable chart format. Our distinct useful features are multi-series editing, spreadsheet mapping, explicit categorical/numeric/date spacing, source notes, native ECharts exports, and clear validation. This is a more competitive expansion, not an easy-ranking claim.

## Remocn assessment

[Remocn](https://github.com/Remocn/remocn) is a copy-in component registry for Remotion video compositions, not a replacement for a statistical chart engine. Its repository includes animated bar and line chart components as well as motion primitives. The [repository license](https://github.com/Remocn/remocn/blob/main/LICENSE) is MIT; copied components need their notices preserved. Remotion is a separate prerequisite with [its own licensing](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license), which must be checked for the actual rendering use case.

This appears most relevant to AnimStats: an animated number/counter maker, animated line-chart clip maker, or animated bar-chart maker. Fit is an inference from its components and AnimStats' current GIF/MP4 positioning, not SEO validation. No Remocn code or dependencies have been installed in ChartsAI.

Before implementation, inspect the exact component, validate the target keyword and expected output, prove reliable browser/server export for typical and worst-case inputs, and estimate the cost of keeping the tool free. Do not add a large video engine to ordinary ChartsAI pages solely to animate their landing page.

## Distribution and learning

Keep tool descriptions, definitions, assumptions and examples in crawlable HTML. Support charts with readable values and explanation; a video alone is not an answer engine resource. Credit SupaMakers honestly, publish useful source and original examples, and make optional sharing easy without forced watermarks or link schemes.

Evaluate search impressions/clicks by landing page, successful tool actions, referring domains, branded interest and actual AI referrals/citations. Citations cannot be guaranteed by schema or `llms.txt`. Expand based on the combination of useful outcomes and observed search opportunities.
