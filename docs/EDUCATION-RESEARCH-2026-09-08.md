# Coordinate plane pilot: search evidence and release scope

Research checked September 8, 2026. Target: Google US, `coordinate plane generator`; synonyms `coordinate graph maker` and `Cartesian plane generator` share one canonical page, `/coordinate-plane-generator/`.

The user's authenticated Ahrefs interface showed 400 estimated US monthly searches and KD 1 (SERP snapshot August 21). Recorded results included PrintableGrid, Math-Aids, Desmos, Oliver Boorman and The Math Worksheet Site. PrintableGrid was reported as DR 0 and Oliver Boorman DR 6. These are third-party estimates, not traffic forecasts or independent current Google ranking verification. Ahrefs still displays a ranking-data repair notice. Primary competitor pages confirm intent for an immediately usable printable grid and point-plotting tool:

- https://printablegrid.com/graph-paper/coordinate-plane
- https://www.math-aids.com/Graph_Paper/Coordinate_Plane_Graph_Paper.html
- https://www.vexplot.com/coordinate-graph-maker
- https://minadastudio.com/generate/coordinate-plane

Distinct deliverable: one tool for a blank plane, labelled-point diagram, plotting worksheet or reading-coordinates exercise. The screen, worksheet and separate answer-key page share the same validated points and equal X/Y unit scale. A4/Letter PDF, SVG/PNG diagram and exact points CSV are included. No account, server upload or model API. Original teaching examples; no curricular accreditation claim.

Validation: range/point limits, signed and fractional coordinates, preservation of zeros and repeated positions, equal coordinate scale, readable labels, hidden-answer behavior, actual exported artifacts and mobile/keyboard usability. Static explanation, original worked example, downloadable grid, canonical and matching SoftwareApplication/Breadcrumb schema. One new indexable route; settings and practice variants do not create indexed URLs.

## Adjacent educational searches

Ahrefs seed screening: `number line maker` 100 estimated US monthly searches (June 3 snapshot; KD unavailable); its result table showed `number line generator` at 800, not independently opened. `quadratic graph calculator` 60 (July 18 snapshot), KD 1, with Desmos, Symbolab and Pearson. No synonym volume summation. Refresh exact clusters and compare UK/India before a new tool family.

Primary pages checked September 8 show that fractions, inequalities and printable number lines already exist (https://figwise.app/number-line-generator and https://mate.tools/number-line-generator). Quadratic sliders and worked properties already exist (https://www.geogebra.org/m/scUHdsQ2). Transformation worksheet generators already exist (https://www.subjectcoach.com/generator/transformations). The prospective benefit is coordinated visual explanation, practice and accurate print/export, not novelty of the mathematical operation.

Next research candidates: number-line generators, slope diagrams and transformation activities. They are not promised or shipped by this pilot. Do not generate routes for arbitrary equations, coordinate ranges, colours, grades or paper sizes.

## Agent skill experiment

Develop a repository chart-quality skill with repeatable data checks and an evaluation protocol. Use it internally first. The deterministic fixtures check correctness; they are not an LLM benchmark and do not prove that a skill improves model output. Public marketing and an installable skill launch require documented baseline/skill-assisted comparisons with identical data and prompts, recorded model/runtime and honest failures. Search demand for agent-chart skills has not been established.
