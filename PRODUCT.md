# ChartsAI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People making charts for articles, newsletters, reports, presentations and assignments; students and educators exploring algebra and geometry; people printing practice sheets or personal habit trackers. There is no established customer base or validated primary demographic. The homepage should make the expanded capabilities easy to discover.

## Product Purpose

Provide valuable free tools and original resources that earn search discovery, use, links and authority for ChartsAI and its maker, SupaMakers. The founder has explicitly prioritized building useful resources while discovery data mature. Paid conversion and social-buzz dependence are outside the current aim.

## Positioning

Visitors can open a working example, enter their own values, inspect the result, and download a usable chart or printable without an account. Visible methods, original examples and public source support verification; do not claim superiority to general-purpose assistants or established math tools.

## Operating Context

Browser-based tasks end in presentation graphics, assignment figures, lesson diagrams or printed sheets. Visitors may arrive directly from search with a specific chart or worksheet need. A homepage visitor needs clear routes into the expanded library.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Eleven statistical and editorial chart makers, including dumbbell, slopegraph and small multiples; publication mode for line and bar charts with annotations, source context, local editable projects, static HTML and image/PDF exports; two sourced editorial examples, twelve editable templates in three example collections, and a publishing guide; five math diagram/calculation tools; a number-line worksheet generator with three original practice packs; a weekly/monthly habit tracker; 50 worked chart examples; 20 guides; five attributed public datasets. Existing Astro/React/ECharts implementation stays browser-local. No login, payment, chat interface or model API dependency. Keep charts mathematically accurate, preview/export consistent, useful defaults visible without JavaScript, and private inputs out of analytics. Preserve canonical URLs and internal linking. The homepage must not load ECharts at runtime.

## Brand Commitments

ChartsAI is made by SupaMakers. Keep truthful attribution and a useful link to SupaMakers. The user requested a homepage redesign using Impeccable to reflect the product expansion; no new logo or sitewide rebrand has been requested.

## Evidence on Hand

Working tools, generated original chart/worksheet assets, source code, calculation tests and dated search research in the repository. No verified adoption metrics, customer quotes, awards or independent learning-outcome claims are available.

## Product Principles

- Earn discovery through useful resources and clear answers.
- Show real outputs and direct actions rather than vague AI promises.
- Preserve values and explain meaningful constraints.
- Make free use and download journeys understandable on mobile.
- Build authority through transparent methods, original examples and open source.
